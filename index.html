Westbound

Every westbound, counted

Westbound is a free iPhone app that logs your drives on Colorado's I-70 mountain corridor and turns them into a sticker collection: for the passes you cross, the streaks you keep, and the hours you put in.

Free on the App Store

You know the passes by heart, and the traffic by heartbreak.

Westbound has 43 stickers for the passes you cross, the streaks you keep, the weather you drive through and the traffic you sit in. Then there are nine Summits, the hard ones, for a lifetime of corridor miles.

How it works

Drive

Tap Start Drive, or turn on auto-detect and let Westbound start and stop for you.

Count

Westbound matches your route against 12 stretches of road. Finish one end to end and it's logged with its direction, east or west. Route matching works with no signal, past the tunnel and beyond.

Collect

Stickers land as you earn them. Stats keeps the numbers for every segment you've driven, including your worst-ever crawl.
